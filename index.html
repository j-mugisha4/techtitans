About Us

Youth-Led Education Innovation

Tech-Titans E-Lab Group is a dynamic youth-led education think tank addressing the literacy crisis among primary students in Gasabo District, Rwanda through innovative teacher training and student reading programs.

Our Mission

To tackle the severe literacy crisis among Primary students in Gasabo District, we propose a two-phase intervention beginning with the empowerment of teachers. First, a specialized English Training Program will be introduced for public-school teachers with support from NGOs and government partners. This hybrid (virtual and in-person) program will provide practical, interactive lessons led by fluent English volunteers and trainers. Through a structured curriculum, teachers will gain strong communication skills and teaching confidence, earning a certificate that validates English proficiency and enhances employability.

Following this, Weekly English Reading Clubs will be launched in public schools to strengthen learners’ reading and comprehension. These clubs will offer small-group sessions featuring guided reading, storytelling, phonics, and learning games, facilitated by trained teachers and community volunteers. Using age-appropriate materials and creative tools, the program will make literacy engaging and inclusive. By improving both teacher capacity and student learning, this initiative will build a sustainable reading culture, boost academic outcomes, and open greater educational opportunities for all children in Gasabo.

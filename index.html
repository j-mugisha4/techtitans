About Us

Problem Statement

In Gasabo District, there is a critical crisis of literacy where in in the public schools, according to UNICEF 2025,almost 90 percent of the students in Primary 3, especially in rural and low-income districts, cannot read or understand grade-level English, the official language of instruction. This is not only a social and educational equity challenge that is core to SDG 4 but also a result of systemic inadequacies such as overcrowded classes, insufficient resources and teacher training to provide students with an opportunity to further lag behind in all topics. To address this underlying factor, we recommend the introduction of weekly English Reading Clubs in partner schools to offer small-group literacy assistance by using guided reading and learning games. The intervention is expected to directly enhance reading fluency and confidence in students, resulting in a positive academic outcome throughout the curriculum and, consequently, bridging the gap in educational disadvantage to broaden their future opportunities.

Our Mission

Our think tank mission is to assist children of Gasabo community in building strong foundational skills by using our interests in education innovation and our capabilities in app development in order to empower them to succeed academically and create their own opportunities.

Complete Solutions Statement

To tackle the severe literacy crisis among Primary students in Gasabo District, we propose a two-phase intervention beginning with the empowerment of teachers.

Phase 1: Weekly English Reading Clubs

Following this, Weekly English Reading Clubs will be launched in public schools to strengthen learners' reading and comprehension. These clubs will offer small-group sessions featuring guided reading, storytelling, phonics, and learning games, facilitated by trained teachers and community volunteers. Using age-appropriate materials and creative tools, the program will make literacy engaging and inclusive.

Phase 2: Specialized English Training Program

A specialized English Training Program will be introduced for public-school teachers with support from NGOs and government partners. This hybrid (virtual and in-person) program will provide practical, interactive lessons led by fluent English volunteers and trainers. Through a structured curriculum, teachers will gain strong communication skills and teaching confidence, earning a certificate that validates English proficiency and enhances employability.

Expected Impact

By improving both teacher capacity and student learning, this initiative will build a sustainable reading culture, boost academic outcomes, and open greater educational opportunities for all children in Gasabo.
